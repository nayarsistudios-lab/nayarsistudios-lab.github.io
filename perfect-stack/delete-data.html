Delete Your Data — Perfect Stack

Perfect Stack · Nayprek Studios · Last updated: October 30, 2026

What the game stores

Perfect Stack does not ask you to create an account, and Nayprek Studios does not run its own servers. Your game data is kept in these places:

  • On your phone: best score, best combo, games played, achievements, the skins and purchases you own, your progress toward unlocking skins, and your settings.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (only if you are signed in): a backup copy of the same progress, stored in your Google account.
  • Google services used by the game: Google Play (purchases) and Google AdMob (ads).

1. Delete the data on your phone

Choose one:

  • Uninstall Perfect Stack, or
  • Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Perfect Stack → Storage and tap Clear data.

This removes all progress stored on the phone. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the cloud copy is not removed by this step (see below).

2. Delete your cloud save

Your cloud save is stored by Google Play Games in your Google account. To delete it:

  • Open the Google Play Games settings on your phone (in the Play Games app, or in the Play Store under your profile).
  • Find the option to delete your Play Games data and choose Perfect Stack.
  • Confirm the deletion.

The exact menu names can differ between phones and Play Games versions. You can also manage or delete your Google account data at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

3. Advertising

To reset or delete your advertising ID, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads and skin packs) are handled and recorded by Google Play, not by Nayprek Studios. Deleting your game data does not cancel or remove a purchase: after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases in the game's Shop to get them back. For purchase records or refunds, see your Google Play order history.

Need help?

If you have any question about your data, or want us to help with a deletion request, email nayprekstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Perfect Stack data deletion". We reply within 30 days.
